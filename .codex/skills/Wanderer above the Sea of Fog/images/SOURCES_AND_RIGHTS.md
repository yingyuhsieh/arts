## Accuracy notes

- The Hamburger Kunsthalle currently catalogues the painting as about 1817; the frequently repeated date 1818 is not certain.
- The painting is unsigned and undated, but the museum continues to attribute it to Caspar David Friedrich. Open research questions are not evidence that the museum has declared it a forgery.
- The wanderer has not been securely identified. Do not label him as Friedrich, Goethe, a soldier or a forestry official as an established fact.
- The landscape is a studio composite. Place names on IMG-004 are tentative scholarly proposals, and the diagram is not a literal viewpoint or a survey map.
- The provenance timeline marks the long pre-1938 gap as unresolved. It does not claim either proven Nazi-era looting or a fully cleared history.

## Requested assets not redistributed in this ZIP

- Ludwig Grote's 1950 article and the 1959 Tate exhibition record are modern archival materials. Reusable reproduction permission was not established, so facsimiles are not included.
- Modern museum screenshots, exhibition photographs and collection-record captures are not included because their reuse terms were not established.
- IMG-010 communicates the documented timeline without reproducing those archival pages.
