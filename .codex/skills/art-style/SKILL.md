# Art Style

Identify the most characteristic visual qualities of the supplied artist or artwork and translate them into practical background direction for a YouTube slide.

Prioritize the specific artwork when one is supplied; otherwise use the artist's established style. Base the direction on visible or well-supported traits rather than inventing motifs. Include the most useful combination of palette, texture or medium, shapes or motifs, spatial treatment, and mood. Keep the background subordinate to artwork, titles, and subtitles by preserving calm negative space and sufficient contrast.

Return exactly one sentence in English, regardless of the input language. Write it as a direct design instruction, with no heading, bullets, alternatives, biography, explanation, or image generation. Do not ask follow-up questions when the artist or artwork is identifiable from the input.
