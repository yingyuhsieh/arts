## Accuracy notes

- The Louvre painting is not the Burlington House Cartoon. The painting includes a lamb; the cartoon includes the young John the Baptist.
- The cartoon described in Fra' Pietro da Novellara's 1501 letter is lost. IMG-004, IMG-005 and IMG-006 must not be presented as that lost sheet.
- The Commons title for IMG-006 refers to a lamb, while descriptive metadata on the same page varies in its subject identification. It is therefore labelled only as a related composition study here.
- The Vespucci note is contemporary documentary evidence, but it does not describe the completed Louvre painting.

## Requested assets not redistributed in this ZIP

- No facsimile of Fra' Pietro's letter or Antonio de Beatis's record is included because a reusable, rights-cleared image was not established.
- Images of the reverse of the panel, infrared discoveries, and the 2009–2012 conservation campaign are modern documentary or technical photographs. They are not included because clear permission for redistribution was not established.
- The research context for those technical images is available from [C2RMF](https://c2rmf.fr/actualite/la-sainte-anne-une-restauration-majeure). Their omission must not be filled by relabelling an ordinary photograph of the painting as an infrared or conservation image.
