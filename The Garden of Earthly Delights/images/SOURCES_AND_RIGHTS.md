## Interpretation cautions

- The center panel has no single scholarly consensus: moral warning, primeval or unfallen humanity, and courtly conversation remain competing approaches.
- The outward-looking man is not securely identified as Bosch, Adam, or the patron.
- The Tree-Man is not a confirmed self-portrait.
- The bird-headed figure's common modern nickname is not a title supplied by Bosch.
- The M on the blade has no securely established meaning.
