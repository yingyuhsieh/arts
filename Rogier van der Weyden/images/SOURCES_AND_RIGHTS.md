# Rogier van der Weyden — sources and image rights

These files are downloaded Wikimedia Commons originals, named in the order of the English and Traditional Chinese video artwork introductions. The Google Doc uses smaller proportional display copies.

Commons marks the eleven reproductions as Public domain (PD-Art or an equivalent public-domain mark) and the Philippe de Croÿ image as CC0. Reuse rules for faithful photographs of public-domain art may differ outside the United States; check the linked file page and the rules of your publication location.

The Justice of Trajan and Herkinbald image depicts a surviving tapestry made after the lost paintings. It must not be labelled as a photograph of the destroyed original paintings. The Seven Sacraments may include workshop participation. The Boston Saint Luke is one of multiple surviving versions. The Braque file shows the interior only.
