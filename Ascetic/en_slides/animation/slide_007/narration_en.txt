The Kimbell Art Museum dates The Torment of Saint Anthony to fourteen eighty-seven, when Michelangelo was about twelve.
Monsters lift Anthony into the air and tug at his clothing. Yet his composed face contrasts with their frantic movement.
Michelangelo adapted Martin Schongauer’s engraving, adding color, animal textures, and a quiet landscape below. Early biographers say he studied fish at a market for inspiration.
The commission remains uncertain. What we can see is a young artist learning through imitation, then making the borrowed image his own. Look from the creatures’ restless bodies back to Anthony’s still expression.
