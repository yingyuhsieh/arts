Our final works approach the edges of asceticism. Serapion is remembered as a martyr. Violence inflicted on him must be distinguished from chosen poverty or self-discipline.
Mary Magdalen’s familiar image as a penitent also needs context: it developed through later religious traditions, beyond the Gospel accounts.
Keep those differences in mind as we move from a white habit to a candle flame. Both paintings suggest deep religious commitment, but their subjects arrive there through different stories. Looking carefully includes understanding which story we are seeing.
