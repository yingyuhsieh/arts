I began with a word. Now ascetic brings many images to mind: Anthony enduring turmoil, Francis choosing poverty, Jerome studying, and Paul welcoming a guest.
Serapion and Magdalen remind us that chosen discipline, inflicted suffering, and later traditions of repentance need different contexts.
Through light, cloth, engraved lines, and bodies, these artists make inward commitments visible.
Next time you look at a painting, pause over one small action: hands in prayer, a head over a book, a face toward light. Where is that person’s attention, and what are they trying to sustain?
