## 主要參考資料

### 查核與延伸閱讀

1. [Vatican Museums：Sistine Chapel — History](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/storia-cappella-sistina.html)。禮拜堂沿革、十五世紀裝飾、《最後的審判》與全面修復年代。
2. [Vatican Museums：Central Stories](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/storie-centrali.html)。九幅中央場景與分組。
3. [The Metropolitan Museum of Art：Studies for the Libyan Sibyl](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/337497)。約1510–1511年習作、模特兒、技法與施工分期；館藏編號24.197.2。
4. [Vatican Museums：Creation of Adam](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/storie-centrali/creazione-di-adamo.html)。作品圖像與館方解說；指尖空隙的描述以畫面觀察為準。
5. [Vatican Museums：Original Sin and Banishment from the Garden of Eden](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/storie-centrali/peccato-originale-e-cacciata-dal-paradiso-terrestre.html)。原罪與放逐場景。
6. [Vatican Museums：The Flood](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/storie-centrali/diluvio-universale.html)。洪水場景。
7. [Vatican Museums：Sibyls and Prophets](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/sibille-e-profeti.html)。先知、西比拉與約拿的圖像位置。
8. [Vatican Museums：Spandrels](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/vele.html)。基督祖先與拱肩配置。
9. [Vatican Museums：Pendentives](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta/pennacchi.html)。四角拯救故事。
10. [Smarthistory：Michelangelo, Ceiling of the Sistine Chapel](https://smarthistory.org/michelangelo-ceiling-of-the-sistine-chapel-2/)；[Web Gallery of Art：Ignudi](https://www.wga.hu/html_m/m/michelan/3sistina/2ignudi/index.html)。構圖、裸體青年與觀看分析。
11. [Gianluigi Colalucci：Michelangelo Buonarroti: Restoration of the Frescoes on the Vaulted Ceiling and the Last Judgment in the Sistine Chapel](https://doi.org/10.6092/issn.1973-9494/7166)，*Conservation Science in Cultural Heritage* 16(1)，89–126。修復主持者的技術報告與方法立場。
12. Ascanio Condivi，*Vita di Michelagnolo Buonarroti*（1553）；Giorgio Vasari，*Lives of the Most Excellent Painters, Sculptors, and Architects*（1568年版相關傳記）。延伸閱讀：陵墓委託、工作困難與競爭軼事；本文將其敘事與已核實的官方工程年代區分。
13. [Vatican Museums：Ceiling](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/collezioni/musei/cappella-sistina/volta.html)。1504年裂縫、1508年委託、原始方案與完成時間。
14. [The Metropolitan Museum of Art：The Papacy and the Vatican Palace](https://www.metmuseum.org/ja/essays/the-papacy-and-the-vatican-palace)。儒略二世的藝術贊助及拉斐爾、米開朗基羅的同期工程。
15. [Vatican Museums：The Sistine Chapel Twenty Years Later — New Breath, New Light（2014）](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/dam/museivaticani/pdf/eventi_novita/iniziative/eventi/2014/convegno_sistina_guida_en.pdf)。修復年表、日本電視台合作與環境保存，尤見卷末年表。
16. [Vatican：Sistine Chapel](https://www.vatican.va/content/vatican/en/ra/cappella-sistina.html)。建築尺寸、教宗選舉用途與工程概況。
17. [Vatican Museums：Saint Paul Preaching at Athens](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en/eventi-e-novita/iniziative/mostre/2026/predica-san-paolo-atene.html)。相關掛毯在1527年遭劫、後來返回梵蒂岡的歷史。
18. Frank Lynn Meshberger, [“An Interpretation of Michelangelo’s Creation of Adam Based on Neuroanatomy”](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1990.03450140059034), *JAMA* 264(14), 1990, pp. 1837–1841。大腦圖像假說的原始提出者。
19. ArtWatch UK, [“The Sistine Chapel Restorations: Part I ~ Setting the Scene, Packing Them In”](https://artwatch.org.uk/the-sistine-chapel-restorations-part-i-setting-the-scene-packing-them-in/), 2013年1月21日。清潔修復的批評方觀點。
